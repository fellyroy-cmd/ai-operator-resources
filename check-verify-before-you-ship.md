# Verify before you ship: the 3-step AI-output checklist

*From my "This 3-step checklist stops you from ever shipping AI slop" short. — Dara*

On Oct 1, 2026 Google froze its open-source bug bounty program after a flood of AI-written reports, most of them invalid or hallucinated. Nobody checked the work before sending it. Run these 3 steps on **anything** AI made before it goes out with your name on it.

## Step 1: Check the facts (2 minutes)

- [ ] Every **number, date, name and quote**: can I find it in a real source? Open the link, don't trust the summary.
- [ ] Every **link**: click it. AI invents URLs.
- [ ] Every **"studies show" / "experts say"**: which study? Which expert? No answer = delete it.

## Step 2: Check it actually works (2 minutes)

- [ ] **Code:** I ran it. It did the thing. Not "it looks right".
- [ ] **Steps / instructions:** I followed them myself, once, start to finish.
- [ ] **Claims about a tool:** I opened the tool and saw the feature exists today.

## Step 3: Check it sounds like you (1 minute)

- [ ] Read it **out loud**. Would you say this to a friend?
- [ ] Cut the AI tells: "delve", "in today's fast-paced world", "it's important to note", "game-changer", three-item lists everywhere.
- [ ] Can you **defend every sentence** if someone asks "how do you know?"

## The rule

**If you can't verify it, cut it or flag it.** "I couldn't confirm X" is more trustworthy than a confident guess.

Five minutes of checking is the difference between "uses AI well" and "AI slop".
