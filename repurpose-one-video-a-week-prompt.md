# The repurpose prompt: 1 video → a week of content

*The exact prompt from my "I turn one video into a week of content" short. — Dara*

**How to use it:** fill in the `[BRAND VOICE]` box once and save it. Every time you post a video, paste the prompt + your script into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. You get 5 platforms back in one go.

---

```
You are my content repurposing assistant. Turn the video script below into
posts for 5 platforms. Keep my voice the same everywhere.

[BRAND VOICE] (fill this in once, then reuse it)
- Who I am: [one line, e.g. "I teach busy creators to use AI tools"]
- Who I talk to: [your audience in one line]
- How I sound: [3 words, e.g. "clear, direct, friendly"]
- Words I use: [2-3 phrases you actually say]
- Words I never use: [e.g. "game-changer", "delve", hype]
- My call to action: [e.g. "Join my free community: LINK"]

RULES
- Use only facts that are in the script. Don't add numbers or claims.
- Every post opens with a hook in the first line. No warm-up.
- Short sentences. Plain words. Sound like a person, not a brand.

WRITE
1. X / TWITTER THREAD: 6-8 tweets. Tweet 1 is the hook. One idea per tweet.
   Last tweet = my call to action.
2. LINKEDIN POST: first line is the insight. 4-6 short paragraphs with line
   breaks. End with a question that invites comments. 3-4 hashtags.
3. INSTAGRAM CAROUSEL: 8 slides. Slide 1 = bold hook (max 8 words).
   Slides 2-7 = one point each (max 20 words). Slide 8 = call to action.
   Then a caption: hook + 3 sentences + 5 hashtags.
4. TIKTOK / REELS SCRIPT: 30-45 seconds. Hook in the first 3 seconds,
   3-5 fast points, call to action. Written to be spoken out loud.
5. NEWSLETTER SECTION: 3 subject line options, a 2-3 sentence intro,
   3-5 bullet takeaways, then a link to the full video.

SCRIPT
[paste your video script here]
```

---

## Make it better over time

- After a week, look at which post did best. Add one line to `[BRAND VOICE]` about why ("short hooks with a number work for me").
- If something sounds off, don't rewrite the post. Fix the voice box, so every future post gets better.
