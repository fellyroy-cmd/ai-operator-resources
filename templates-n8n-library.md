280+ ready-made n8n workflows with 25,000+ GitHub stars. They cover Gmail, Telegram, Slack, Discord, WhatsApp, Google Drive, Notion and OpenAI, and include AI agents, RAG chatbots, email automation, social media and document processing.

## Use one in 4 steps

1. **Pick a template** from the repo's list. Start with something boring and useful, like "summarise new emails" or "save attachments to Drive".
2. **Download its `.json` file.**
3. **In n8n:** open a new workflow → the **⋯ menu** → **Import from file** → choose the JSON.
4. **Connect your accounts** on each node with a red warning (Gmail, OpenAI, etc.), change the 1–2 settings that are yours (which inbox, which folder), then **Test workflow**.

## Don't have n8n yet?

- **Free to self-host**, or try **n8n Cloud** (free trial) at [n8n.io](https://n8n.io).

## Two tips

- **Read the workflow before you switch it on.** Know what it sends and where.
- **Use separate API keys with a spending limit** for anything that calls an AI model.
