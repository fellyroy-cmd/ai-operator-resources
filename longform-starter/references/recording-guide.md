# Recording guide (for the creator)

Read `teleprompter.md` (one sentence per line) off your phone or a prompter app. The cut does the rest, but only if you
record in a way it can understand.

## Set-up (once)
- **16:9, landscape**, 1080p or 4K, **30 fps**. Medium shot: head + shoulders + a bit of desk, eyes about a third from the top.
- Sit slightly left or right of centre. The free side is where graphics appear beside you.
- Face lit from the front-side, the room visible behind you (it's part of the look). Same spot every video = consistency.
- Leave headroom: punch-ins zoom 1.24x on your face, so don't sit at the very top of the frame.

## Microphone
- **Phone/camera mic only:** fine. Skip sync, it's one file.
- **Separate mic:** sync it to the camera audio in any editor first, then drop the synced file in `raw/`.

## How to read
- One sentence at a time. **Mess up → pause 1 second → restart the WHOLE sentence** (not mid-sentence). The cut keeps your
  LAST clean take of every line automatically.
- Say lines you're happy with once and keep going. No need to say "cut" or clap between takes.
- Small natural ad-libs are fine. They show up in `cut/takes.md` as "unscripted stretches" and can be rescued.
- Energy: a notch above conversation. Smile on the hook. Look into the lens, not at the prompter text, on the hook and the CTA.
- Pause ~1 second between chapters (it makes clean section cuts).

## After
Drop the file(s) into `videos/<video>/raw/` and say "I recorded it". Claude runs the cut and the beat map, and
you approve the beat map before anything gets built.
