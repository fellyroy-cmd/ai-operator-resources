# 01 · Script

A great edit can't save a confusing video. The script is the product; everything visual exists to make the words land.

## Structure (9–11 min, ~1,400–1,700 words)
1. **Cold open (≤ 15–20s):** the claim, stated as already true, with the proof on screen in frame one. No "hey guys", no intro.
2. **Belief flip:** "Most people think X, but now Y."
3. **The promise + two open loops:** "I'll walk you through the N [parts]", "along the way, the one [habit] that…",
   "at the end, [the honest number]". Pay off both: the habit in the last chapter, the number in the closing section.
4. **Credibility in one breath + the failure story:** why the obvious way didn't work for the creator. Real details only.
5. **The insight** that unlocked it, in one sentence the viewer can copy today.
6. **Chapters (3–7):** each = name it → why it matters (what breaks without it) → the creator's early mistake → how it works now →
   one concrete number. **One idea per chapter**; over 2.5 min means split it.
7. **Recap list** of the chapters in one line.
8. **The honest numbers:** cost, time, result, compared with the normal way. Then "is it worth it? For me, yes, because…"
9. **CTA:** free resource inside your free community, link in description. Then like + subscribe, one line.

## Mid-video CTA
Right after the creator has shown a real concept working (~3:00–3:30), as a natural "that's exactly what we break down in the
Discord" line. Never before the first chapter pays off.

## Sentences
- Written to be SAID: ≤ 28 words, one breath, contractions. If the creator would stumble reading it aloud, rewrite it.
- Numbers as digits, exactly as spoken ("$445", "12 hours"), so captions, facts and the lint all match.
- Define every term the first time in five words ("a sub-agent is just a helper").

## Never
Listicles of 10 shallow ideas. Unsourced numbers. Hype words ("insane", "game-changer"). A chapter with no number or example.
