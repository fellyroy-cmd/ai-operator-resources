# 08 · Captions and headlines

**Two systems, never both at once:**

## Captions: talking-head shots only (full, punch, overlay, framed)
- White Inter 700 52px, bottom centre (top 902px), no box, a soft shadow. Words appear as they're spoken.
- Chunks ≤ 5 words / 34 chars; break on punctuation, pauses over 0.45s and every beat cut. A chunk disappears before
  the next one appears.
- Numbers and the beat's `emphasis` words in Instrument Serif italic.
- No captions over chapter cards.

## Headlines: graphic scenes (bubble, split, graphic)
- The spoken line's 2–4 key words, top-left (split: above the right column), Inter 800 ~104px, ONE `*accent*` word in
  serif italic.
- Each word blurs in when it's said. If the headline paraphrases instead of quoting (under 60% of its words are
  spoken), it staggers in quickly instead of chasing scattered matches.
- The same headline on consecutive beats holds (no re-animation) while the diagram below keeps building.

Spelling comes from the script, timing from whisper ("Cloud code" → "Claude Code" automatically). If a caption is wrong,
fix the script line, not the transcript.
