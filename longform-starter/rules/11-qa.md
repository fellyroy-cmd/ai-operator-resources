# 11 · QA: check the way a viewer watches

Checking at 1 frame a second misses what a viewer spots instantly. Check at **10 frames a second** around every cut.

The six mistakes (machine-checked by qa.py, then confirmed by eye on the sheets):
1. **An animation plays twice:** the same graphic on neighbouring beats without `hold`.
2. **A graphic flashes:** on screen < 0.9s.
3. **Text too late to read:** lands > 0.25s after its word, lands after the beat ends (never seen), or leaves before it
   can be read (3.2 words/s + 0.4s).
4. **A graphic covers the face:** stage rect vs the on-screen face box; over 8% overlap fails.
5. **A word said twice:** re-transcribe the render; 2+ words twice = FAIL, 1 word twice = listen.
6. **Lips drift from the voice:** render length vs cut length, section frame counts, sync.json residual < 1 frame.
Plus: loudness -14 ±1 LUFS, peak ≤ -1.5, dead air > 1.2s, music ≤ 0.10.

Visual pass extras: two the creators on screen at once, empty panel frames, captions over graphics, black frames at section seams,
clipped chapter text. **The creator never sees a render with a FAIL.**
