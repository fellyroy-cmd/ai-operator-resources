# 03 · Facts

AI will happily invent a fact that sounds right: a view count, a date, a price. So:

- Every number, date, price, ranking, quote and "X did Y" claim gets a row in `facts.md` with a source you can open
  (a file in this HQ, a URL, a screenshot in `assets/img/`).
- **If it can't be proven, cut the line.** Don't soften it into "around" or "about".
- Prefer the creator's own evidence: your own logs, commits, dashboards, render times. Screenshot them for the beat map
  while you're there. A proven fact is also a graphic.
- Counting words ("one job", "two things") aren't claims. `script_lint.py` skips 1/2/3 without $ or %.
- Re-check prices and model names on publish day if the script is more than a week old.
